Purchases (en)

The app includes three days of full access. After that, continued access requires an optional one-time purchase. The trial does not renew or charge automatically, and there is no subscription. Apple or Google processes payment; the developer does not receive payment-card details. Local purchase and trial records help manage access. Restoring purchases may require the store account used for the purchase and an internet connection. On Android, this purchase requirement does not apply while a valid access code is active. A valid code gives you all features for free, with no time limit. Android also stores on the device whether access-code access is active. Identification does not send your photo to a server. Apple and Google services may use a network connection for app or asset downloads, purchase information, purchases, restoration, and purchase verification. Links you open and files you choose to share are handled by the services or destinations you select, under their own policies. These activities are separate from on-device photo matching.
